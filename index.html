Needs the Street View Static API switched on in your Google Cloud account. The key is stored only in this browser. Without it, Street View opens as a link.

Every search already cross-checks Open Charge Map, one of the sources Zapmap itself draws on, using a built-in key. Enter your own key here only if the shared one stops working.

Distances are straight-line. Walking time assumes a route 30% longer than the straight line at 80 m per minute. Your entries are saved in this browser only.
